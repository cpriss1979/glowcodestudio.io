THREE SIDES

Wellness, reflection, and gentle motivation.

Three Sides is a wellness companion designed around the highs, lows, and balance of emotional life. It combines journaling, personalized coping tools, and a virtual pet to make self-care feel more encouraging, consistent, and manageable.

Theme

Choose a space that feels calming.

Customize colors and appearance to create an environment that helps you feel safe, relaxed, and comfortable every time you open the app.

Daily Journal

Track thoughts, moods, and progress.

Quick journal entries help you recognize patterns, celebrate victories, and better understand what influences your emotions over time.

Coping Tools

Support when you need it most.

Save personalized grounding exercises, reminders, calming activities, and coping strategies so they are always easy to find.

Virtual Pet Motivation

Small habits. Big encouragement.

Take care of your virtual companion by taking care of yourself. Completing healthy habits helps your pet grow while encouraging consistency through positive reinforcement.

Three Sides turns small acts of self-care into visible progress, helping users build supportive routines without making wellness feel like another chore.

AMYGDALA

Calming support for difficult moments.

Amygdala is a personal anxiety companion designed to make overwhelming moments feel more manageable. Instead of burying users in information, it offers focused exercises, calming tools, hope, and practical support that can be reached quickly.

Panicky

Ground yourself when anxiety spikes.

A guided reset exercise helps slow the moment down, reconnect you with your surroundings, and make intense anxiety feel more manageable.

Hope

Encouragement when you feel low or numb.

Carefully selected Bible verses offer comfort, reassurance, and a reminder that difficult moments do not last forever.

Thoughts

Create order when your mind feels crowded.

A simple organizing exercise helps separate racing thoughts, identify what needs attention, and bring greater clarity to the moment.

Breathe

Slow your breathing and calm your body.

Guided breathing helps ease physical tension, settle the nervous system, and return your focus to the present.

Coping Tools

Keep helpful strategies close.

Save personal reminders, grounding activities, calming ideas, and techniques that work specifically for you.

Emergency Contacts

Reach support without searching for it.

Keep trusted friends, family members, and important support resources ready for the moments when asking for help feels hardest.

Anxiety does not have to control the entire day. Amygdala brings practical calming tools, grounding exercises, faith-based encouragement, and personal support into one focused companion.
