Amygdala

An anxiety-support companion built around practical, personalized tools.

Amygdala is your personal anxiety companion designed to make difficult moments feel more manageable. Instead of overwhelming you with information, it offers calming tools, guided exercises, journaling, and personalized coping strategies that are there whenever you need them.

Emergency Contacts

Support is easier to reach.

Quickly reach trusted friends, family, or crisis resources when you need support the most.

Grounding Exercises

Return to the present moment.

Interactive activities help calm racing thoughts and gently bring your attention back to what is happening right now.

Breathing Exercises

Slow your body down.

Simple guided breathing sessions help reduce stress and ease some of the physical sensations that can come with anxiety.

Anxiety Toolkit

Keep what works close by.

Create a personalized collection of coping skills, reminders, comforting photos, music, and techniques that work specifically for you.

Anxiety does not have to control your day. Amygdala combines journaling, coping tools, grounding exercises, and positive habit building into one calming companion designed to help you feel more in control—one small step at a time.

Three Sides

A wellness app inspired by the highs, lows, and balance of bipolar life.

Three Sides turns everyday self-care into something gentler and more encouraging. It helps users notice patterns, save coping strategies, track progress, and care for a virtual companion while building healthier habits.

Theme

Choose a space that feels calming.

Customize colors and appearance to create an environment that helps you feel safe, relaxed, and comfortable every time you open the app.

Daily Journal

Track thoughts, moods, and progress.

Quick journal entries help you recognize patterns, celebrate victories, and better understand what influences your mood over time.

Coping Tools

Support when you need it most.

Save personalized grounding exercises, breathing techniques, reminders, and calming activities so they are always just one tap away during stressful moments.

Virtual Pet Motivation

Small habits. Big encouragement.

Take care of your virtual companion by taking care of yourself. Completing healthy habits helps your pet grow while encouraging consistency through positive reinforcement.

Three Sides brings mood awareness, coping support, journaling, and positive motivation together in one personal space—without making self-care feel clinical or overwhelming.
