What we collect on this Site

  • No account is required. Browsing this Site does not require you to register or create an account.

05

Information stored on your device

Some mini-tools may save preferences, settings, or progress directly inside your browser. This information remains on your device unless a particular tool clearly explains otherwise.

You can remove locally stored information by clearing the website’s cookies and site storage through your browser settings.

06

Your choices

  • Delete local information: clear the Site’s data or storage in your browser to remove locally saved settings and entries.
  • Ask a question: contact us if you would like more information about how the Site handles information.
  • Request deletion: you may request deletion of personal information you sent directly to Glow Code Studio, such as information contained in an email or project inquiry.
07

Children’s privacy

The Site is intended for general audiences and is not directed to children under 13.

If you believe a child provided personal information directly to Glow Code Studio, please contact us so that information can be deleted.

If Glow Code Studio adds optional features such as account sign-in, cloud storage, or cloud synchronization, this policy will be updated to explain what information is collected, why it is needed, and the choices available to you.

We may also update this policy when the Site or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
